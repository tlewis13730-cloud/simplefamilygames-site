Support

Need a hand?

Simple Family Games is currently in development. Feedback from families and testers helps shape each release.

Contact Lew Labs Apps

For bug reports, gameplay questions, accessibility feedback, or general suggestions, email us and include the device you are using plus a short description of what happened.

hello@lewlabsapps.com

Helpful details to include

  • Your device model and Android version.
  • The game and mode you were using.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue.

Current release scope

The first release is focused on Tic-Tac-Toe with one-player and local two-player play, multiple computer difficulty levels, and match formats. Memory Match is planned as the next game module.
