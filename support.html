Support

Need a hand?

We welcome gameplay questions, bug reports, and accessibility feedback from families and testers.

Contact Lew Labs Apps

Email us with the device you are using, the game and mode, and a short description of what happened.

hello@lewlabsapps.com

Helpful details to include

  • Device model and Android version.
  • Simple Family Games version 1.7.0, if that is the installed version.
  • The game, player mode, and difficulty or board size.
  • What you expected, what happened, and whether it happens again.
  • A screenshot with no private notifications visible, if useful.

Current release

The version 1.7 launch candidate contains 22 complete games, Family Game Night, Trophy Room, Save & Continue, Candy Pop and Neon Energy modes, home background and music choices, original offline audio, and optional spoken guides.

Quick troubleshooting

  • Open the gear to adjust game sounds, music, narration, game pace, or Reduced Motion.
  • Use the pause button in a game to save, return home, restart, or reopen its lesson.
  • If audio is silent, check both the app’s audio switches and the device media volume.
  • If a saved game is no longer wanted, remove it from the Continue Playing card on the home screen.

Privacy

Simple Family Games is offline, account-free, ad-free, and analytics-free. Read the privacy policy for details.
