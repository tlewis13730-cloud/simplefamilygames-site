Privacy

Built to keep things simple.

The initial Simple Family Games release is designed to work without accounts, advertising, analytics, or online social features.

Initial release privacy design

The current Simple Family Games build is intentionally offline-first and minimizes data collection.

  • No user account or login.
  • No advertising SDK.
  • No analytics SDK.
  • No location, microphone, camera, contacts, Bluetooth, or advertising-ID access.
  • No server or network API is required for gameplay.
  • No chat, user-generated content, or social feed.
  • Player names used during play are not intended to be sent to Lew Labs Apps.

Children and families

Simple Family Games is being developed for family use. Any future feature that introduces purchases, advertising, analytics, external links, or online functionality will require a new privacy and store-policy review before release.

Future changes

This policy will be updated if the final production app changes how information is handled. The Google Play Data safety, target-audience, content-rating, and Families declarations will be completed against the final shipping version and every included SDK.
