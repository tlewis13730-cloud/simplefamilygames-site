Privacy

Made for play, not tracking.

Simple Family Games works offline and is designed without accounts, advertising, analytics, or personal-data collection.

What the app collects

Lew Labs Apps does not collect, receive, sell, share, or use personal information from Simple Family Games. The app does not include an account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking SDK, chat, social feed, or cloud gameplay service.

Information kept on the device

Game settings, optional player names, favorites, recent games, Trophy Room progress, Family Game Night progress, and Save & Continue sessions are stored locally on the device. They are not sent to Lew Labs Apps. Android can remove this local information when the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared.

Permissions and network use

  • No location, microphone, camera, contacts, Bluetooth, or advertising-ID access.
  • No network connection is required for gameplay, artwork, music, or game sounds.
  • Optional spoken guides use the speech service configured on the device. Simple Family Games does not receive the spoken text or speech-service data.
  • The app contains no in-app purchases, external advertising, or real-money features.

Children and families

The app is designed for children and families. It contains no user-generated content, messaging, public profiles, or behaviorally targeted content. Privacy, support, and audio-credit information inside the app is placed behind a three-second grown-up gate.

Future changes

If a future release changes the app’s data practices, this policy and the applicable store disclosures will be updated before that release. The current policy covers the twenty-two-game version 1.7 launch candidate.
