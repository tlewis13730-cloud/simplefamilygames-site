Privacy by design

Family games without the data maze.

Simple Family Games is designed for offline play with no account, advertising, analytics, chat, or social feed.

Plain-language summary

The current Simple Family Games build is intentionally offline-first. Lew Labs Apps does not require a player account and does not add advertising, analytics, online chat, or social features to the app.

The short version

Game choices, favorites, recent games, preferences, and supported scores stay on the device. They are not intended to be sent to Lew Labs Apps.

Information the app does not collect

The current app does not include features intended to collect or transmit personal information during gameplay.

  • No user account, profile, or login.
  • No advertising SDK or advertising identifier use.
  • No analytics SDK or behavior tracking.
  • No purchases or payment information.
  • No chat, social feed, or user-generated content sharing.
  • No remote server or network API required for gameplay.

Device permissions

The current gameplay design does not need access to location, microphone, camera, contacts, Bluetooth, or the device's advertising identifier. If a future feature changes the permissions the app needs, this policy and the applicable store disclosures will be reviewed before that feature is released.

Settings and progress stored on the device

The app may save small pieces of game information locally so it can remember how the family likes to play. This can include:

  • The selected Neon Energy or Candy Pop theme.
  • Sound, background music, vibration, spoken-guide, game-pace, Reduce Motion, and other grown-up settings.
  • Game appearance choices, supported personal-best scores, and game preferences.
  • Favorite games and up to eight recently opened games.

This information stays on the device and is not tied to an online account. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app may remove it.

This website

This is a static informational website. Lew Labs Apps does not add advertising, analytics, account sign-in, or contact forms to it. The color-theme switch can save a simple Neon Energy or Candy Pop preference in the browser so the site can remember the visitor's choice.

The website's hosting provider may process standard technical request information needed to deliver the pages, such as an IP address, browser details, and request time, under the provider's own policies. Lew Labs Apps does not use that information to build player profiles.

Links to Lew Labs Apps or an email program leave this website. Information a person chooses to send by email is used to read and respond to that message.

Children and families

Simple Family Games is made for shared family use. The app avoids account creation, online communication, targeted advertising, and social features. A grown-up should send support emails or other messages outside the app on a child's behalf.

Future changes

This policy will be updated if a production release changes how information is handled. Any future feature that introduces advertising, analytics, purchases, external accounts, or online functionality will require a new privacy and store-policy review before release.

Questions about privacy

Questions about this policy or the app's privacy design can be sent to Lew Labs Apps at hello@lewlabsapps.com.
